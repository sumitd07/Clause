SYNTH-01 Individual Health Policy

3 Benefits

3.2 Room rent limit
Room rent is payable up to 1% of the sum insured per day. If the insured person occupies a room with a higher daily rent, the room rent and the associated medical expenses shall be paid in the same proportion as the eligible room rent bears to the actual room rent. Associated medical expenses means doctor fees, nursing charges and operation theatre charges. Pharmacy, consumables, implants and diagnostics are not associated medical expenses for this clause.

3.5 Cataract sub-limit
Claims for cataract treatment are payable up to Rs 40,000 per hospitalisation.

4 Waiting periods

4.1 Initial waiting period
No claim is payable for any hospitalisation in the first 30 days from the policy start date, except hospitalisation following an accident.

4.2 Specific waiting period
Treatment of cataract, hernia and joint replacement is not payable until 24 months (730 days) from the policy start date.

4.3 Pre-existing diseases
Expenses for any pre-existing disease declared in the proposal are not payable until 36 months (1095 days) from the policy start date.

5 Exclusions

5.1 Cosmetic surgery
Cosmetic or aesthetic treatment is excluded, unless it is reconstruction following an accident or burns.

5.2 Non-medical items
Non-medical items such as attendant charges, toiletries, food for attendants and registration charges are not payable.

6 Cost sharing

6.1 Co-payment for senior citizens
If the insured person is aged 61 or above on the date of admission, each admissible claim is subject to a co-payment of 20% of the admissible amount.

E1 Endorsement: Room rent waiver
Where the room rent waiver rider is purchased, clause 3.2 does not apply and room rent is payable as billed.
